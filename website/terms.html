01

Service Description

Phosphor provides system-wide URL filtering for iOS using Apple's NEURLFilterManager API. The service filters URLs across Safari and other apps to block ads, trackers, malware, and unwanted content using on-device Bloom filters and encrypted Private Information Retrieval (PIR) queries.

No Guarantee of Filtering

While Phosphor aims to block known unwanted URLs, we do not guarantee that all ads, trackers, malware, or unwanted content will be blocked. URL filtering depends on:

  • The completeness and accuracy of the filter lists
  • The availability of the PIR server
  • Apple's OHTTP relay availability
  • iOS system behavior and limitations

Phosphor is not a replacement for antivirus software, a VPN, or safe browsing practices.

05

Service Availability

We strive to maintain high availability of the PIR server and related infrastructure. However, the service may be temporarily unavailable due to maintenance, updates, or factors beyond our control. When the PIR server is unavailable, the on-device Bloom filter continues to function, and URLs that don't match the Bloom filter are allowed through (fail-open behavior).
